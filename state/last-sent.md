_Google Search Status Dashboard · August 18, 2026_

*What happened*
Google released the August 2026 spam update on August 18, 2026. It rolled out globally, in all languages, and finished on August 21, 2026.

*What the terms mean*
Spam update (an algorithm change Google runs to push down low-quality or manipulative pages in search results, and correspondingly lift genuinely useful ones).

*Why it matters for your content*
If any of your pages lean on thin, auto-generated, or keyword-stuffed content, they were at risk of losing rankings during this window. Pages built on genuine expertise and clear answers were more likely to hold or gain visibility.

*Example*
Say you have a "best CRM software" comparison page written mostly by scraping competitor lists. That page could have dropped in rankings August 18-21. A page with original testing, screenshots, and a clear verdict was more likely to stay stable or rise.
