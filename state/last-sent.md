*🟠 Bing adds topic-level view of AI search presence*
_Bing Webmaster Blog · October 13, 2026_

*Synopsis*
Bing's own tool update giving site owners a beta dashboard of where their pages get cited across AI Search topic clusters.
· New "Topics Portfolio" view groups a site's pages by subject and shows presence across AI Search.
· Each topic shows Topic Size, Sites Cited, and Queries — demand, competition, and activity signals.
· Topic pages split grounding queries (where you're already cited) from Opportunity Queries (where rivals are cited instead).
· Bing says the AI classifications and numbers are approximate — treat as directional, not exact.

*What the terms mean*
AI Search — Bing's AI-generated answer surface, similar to Google's AI Overviews.
Topics Portfolio — new beta view in Bing Webmaster Tools that groups your pages by subject/topic.
Grounding queries — searches where Bing's AI already cites your pages as a source.
Opportunity Queries — searches within a topic where competitor pages get cited instead of yours.
Topic Size — how much search demand exists inside that topic cluster.
Sites Cited — how many different sites get cited as sources within a topic.

*Why it matters for your content*
This is the first place Bing shows you gaps by topic, not just by keyword — where competitors are getting the citation and you're not. It turns AI visibility from guesswork into something you can prioritise: pick the topic with biggest size and most missed Opportunity Queries, and build content to close that gap.

*Example*
A running-gear retailer checks the "running shoes" topic. Overall it ranks fine, but the Opportunity Queries list shows "best cushioned running shoes for marathon training" is being cited from a competitor, not them. Before: they didn't know this specific query existed as a gap. After: they write a dedicated page targeting that exact phrasing to compete for the citation.

*Do this*
Open Bing Webmaster Tools, find the Topics Portfolio beta, and scan your top topics' Opportunity Queries for gaps worth writing content against.
